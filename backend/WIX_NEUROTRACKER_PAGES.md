# Neurology Solutions Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 July 2026**

Neurology Solutions, under the lead consultancy of Dr Melinda Pascoe, respects
your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act
1988 (Cth), the Australian Privacy Principles and other applicable health-record
requirements.

This policy explains how we collect, hold, use, disclose and protect personal
information, including information submitted through the NeuroTracker Clinical
mobile application.

## Information we collect

We collect information reasonably necessary to provide and administer health
services. Depending on your dealings with us, this may include:

- identity and contact information, including your name, date of birth, address,
  telephone number and email address;
- referral, appointment, billing and Medicare information;
- health information, clinical history, investigation results, correspondence
  and treatment information;
- information you provide through clinic forms, telephone calls, email or our
  website; and
- information submitted through NeuroTracker Clinical, including your name,
  selected neurological condition, chosen symptoms, symptom scores, wellness
  score, check-in date and time, and app-generated identifiers used to associate
  entries with your profile and prevent duplicate submissions.

Health information is sensitive information. We generally collect it directly
from you, your authorised representative, a referring practitioner, another
healthcare provider involved in your care, or through NeuroTracker Clinical with
your consent.

## How we use information

We may use personal information to:

- provide, coordinate and monitor your clinical care;
- communicate with you and healthcare providers involved in your care;
- manage referrals, appointments, accounts and health records;
- review symptom and wellness information submitted through NeuroTracker
  Clinical;
- maintain, secure and troubleshoot our clinical and information systems;
- meet legal, professional, insurance and record-keeping obligations; and
- respond to enquiries, access or correction requests, and complaints.

NeuroTracker Clinical information is not sold and is not used for advertising
or marketing.

## Disclosure of information

We may disclose relevant personal information where reasonably necessary for
your care or practice administration, including to healthcare practitioners and
services involved in your care, authorised representatives, Medicare or
insurers, and contracted service providers that support our secure clinical,
communications or information systems.

We may also disclose information where you have consented, where disclosure is
required or authorised by law, or where necessary to prevent a serious threat
to life, health or safety. We do not disclose health information for direct
marketing without specific consent.

## NeuroTracker Clinical storage and transmission

Your NeuroTracker Clinical profile and check-in history are stored locally on
your device. Check-ins are transmitted over an encrypted connection to a
clinic-controlled system. If transmission fails, the app retains the pending
check-in on your device and retries later.

Resetting or uninstalling the app may remove information stored locally on that
device. It does not delete information already received and retained by the
clinic as part of its health records.

NeuroTracker Clinical is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat,
cure or prevent any medical condition. It is not continuously monitored and
must not be used for urgent or emergency assistance. In an emergency, call 000.

## Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal
information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access,
modification or disclosure. These measures include access controls, passwords,
firewalls, encryption, secure backups and limiting access to authorised people.

No electronic system is completely risk-free. Please protect your device with a
passcode, install security updates and notify us promptly if a device containing
NeuroTracker Clinical information is lost or compromised.

We retain health information for the periods required by applicable laws and
professional obligations. When information is no longer required and we are not
legally required to retain it, we take reasonable steps to destroy or
de-identify it securely.

## Access and correction

You may request access to personal information we hold about you or ask us to
correct information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or
misleading. We may ask you to make the request in writing and verify your
identity. Legal exceptions may apply, and a reasonable access fee may sometimes
be charged where permitted. There is no fee for requesting a correction.

# NeuroTracker Clinical Support

NeuroTracker Clinical allows participating Neurology Solutions patients to
record selected neurological symptoms and a daily wellness score for review by
the clinic.

## Important medical information

NeuroTracker Clinical is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat,
cure or prevent any medical condition. It does not replace professional medical
advice and is not continuously monitored.

Do not use the app for urgent or emergency assistance. In an emergency, call
000. For non-emergency medical concerns, contact your treating practitioner.

## If a check-in shows as pending

Keep the app installed and connect the phone to the internet. Open the app and
tap the sync status, or open **Settings** and use the retry control. A pending
check-in remains stored on the device until it can be sent successfully or the
local app data is reset.

## Notifications

The app uses a local daily reminder. If reminders do not appear:

1. Open **Settings** in NeuroTracker Clinical and confirm the reminder time.
2. Open the phone's system settings and allow notifications for NeuroTracker
   Clinical.
3. Check that battery-saving or focus settings are not suppressing reminders.

## Changing your tracked symptoms

Open **Settings**, select **Edit patient profile**, and update the condition or
symptoms requested by your clinic. Editing a profile does not remove check-ins
already received by the clinic.

## Resetting or uninstalling the app

Resetting the app removes the saved profile and local check-in history from that
device. Uninstalling may also remove local information. Neither action deletes
information already received and retained by the clinic. Contact the clinic for
requests concerning clinic-held information.

## Privacy

Read the Neurology Solutions Privacy Policy:

`https://www.melindapascoeneurology.com/our-privacy-policy`
